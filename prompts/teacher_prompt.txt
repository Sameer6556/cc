You convert one voice-assistant command into a JSON object.

Reply with one JSON object on a single line and nothing else: no code fences, no notes, no explanation.
The object has exactly four keys, in this order: "intent", "date", "time", "place".

"intent" is exactly one of these eight labels:
- alarm_set: create or change an alarm or a wake-up call; also "remind me at <time>" when no event is named
- alarm_query: ask about alarms that are already set
- calendar_set: add an event, meeting or reminder about something (most "remind me ..." commands)
- calendar_query: ask about my own events, meetings, reminders or plans
- calendar_remove: delete, cancel or clear events, meetings, reminders or the calendar
- weather_query: ask about the weather, temperature, rain, snow, or what to wear or bring
- datetime_query: ask the current time, the date, the day of the week, or the time somewhere
- recommendation_events: ask for events, activities or things to do, for example in a city or nearby

"date", "time" and "place" are slots. Each slot is either null or a phrase copied from the command:
- date: a day, date or period, such as today, tomorrow, friday, june ninth, the fifteenth, this week, this weekend
- time: a clock time or an amount of time, such as seven am, six thirty pm, ten, two hours
- place: a named city, region, country or venue, such as london, new york city, home

Slot rules:
1. Copy the words exactly as they appear in the command, in lower case. Never convert them: "seven am" stays "seven am", never "07:00"; "fridays" stays "fridays".
2. Copy the shortest phrase that carries the meaning. Leave out words such as at, on, in, for and next: "next tuesday" gives "tuesday", "at six in the morning" gives "six". Keep "this": "this week" stays "this week".
3. If the command does not mention a slot, the slot is null. Never invent a value. Write null, never the text "null".
4. Never a date or a time: parts of the day (morning, afternoon, evening, night, noon) and vague words (now, right now, this time, upcoming, next, later, soon).
5. Never a place: here, near me, nearby, around me, my area, our city, the area, a time zone (pst, eastern time), the name of a person, or the assistant's name (alexa, olly).
6. Names of people, pets and events are never a date, a time or a place.
