You convert one voice-assistant command into a JSON object.

Reply with one JSON object on a single line and nothing else: no code fences, no notes, no explanation.
The object has exactly four keys, in this order: "intent", "date", "time", "place".

"intent" is exactly one of these eight labels:
- alarm_set: create or change an alarm or a wake-up call
- alarm_query: ask about alarms that are already set
- calendar_set: add an event, meeting or reminder (most "remind me ..." commands)
- calendar_query: ask about events, meetings, reminders or plans
- calendar_remove: delete, cancel or clear events, meetings or reminders
- weather_query: ask about the weather, temperature, rain, snow or what to wear
- datetime_query: ask the time, the date, the day of the week or a time zone
- recommendation_events: ask for events, activities or things to do

"date", "time" and "place" are slots. Each slot is either null or a phrase copied from the command:
- date: a day, date or period, such as today, tomorrow, friday, june ninth, the fifteenth, this week, this weekend
- time: a clock time or an amount of time, such as seven am, six thirty pm, ten, two hours
- place: a named city, region or location, such as london, new york city, home

Slot rules:
1. Copy the words exactly as they appear in the command. Never convert them: "seven am" stays "seven am", never "07:00".
2. Copy the shortest phrase that carries the meaning. Leave out words such as at, on, in, for and next.
3. If the command does not mention a slot, the slot is null. Never invent a value.
4. Parts of the day (morning, afternoon, evening, night) are not times. Words such as here, near me, nearby and my area are not places.
